# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Patients who experience symptoms and want to understand possible conditions and seek care.

## Product Purpose

Pocket Doc helps patients describe symptoms and receive an assessment with possible conditions, explanations, and precautions. Results are not a medical diagnosis. Emergency warnings take priority over the assessment flow.

## Operating Context

Patients can enter symptoms in their own words, select them from a checklist, and answer follow-up questions when needed. They can review saved assessments. Sign-in, a completed profile, and active health data consent are required to submit an assessment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app shows an emergency alert instead of continuing the normal assessment when an emergency is detected.
- Patients can manage their profile and consent in their account.
- Sending reports to doctors and booking appointments are planned, but are not available yet. Do not present them as working features.

## Brand Commitments

The patient web app uses the name Pocket Doc.

## Evidence on Hand

The app's current behavior and copy are in `src/`. Setup and routes are described in `README.md`. Do not invent clinical validation, outcomes, testimonials, or other proof.

## Product Principles

- Make symptom assessment understandable to patients.
- Keep the limits of predictions clear and give emergency warnings priority.
- Respect patient consent and access to personal health information.
- Describe available and planned features accurately.
